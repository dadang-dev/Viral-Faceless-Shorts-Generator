## 🎨 Style Bible (áp dụng cho MỌI ảnh/video để đồng bộ toàn tuần)

Dán đoạn này vào cuối mỗi prompt ảnh/video để giữ phong cách nhất quán:

```
clean minimal flat-design motion graphic style, isometric icons,
navy blue and off-white background, single gold/amber accent color,
soft drop shadows, simple geometric shapes (coins, wallet, charts,
arrows), generous negative space for text overlay, subtle grain
texture, no photorealistic faces, no watermark, 9:16 vertical
```

**Bảng màu cố định:** navy đậm (nền) / trắng ngà (chữ, hình) / vàng gold (điểm nhấn, số liệu quan trọng)
**Phong cách hình:** flat/isometric motion graphic — KHÔNG dùng photorealistic (tránh cả rủi ro AIGC label lẫn tránh cảm giác "giả làm người thật" trong ngách tài chính vốn cần độ tin cậy cao)
**Camera/chuyển động:** icon/chart tự chuyển động, số liệu đếm tăng dần, chữ trượt vào khung — không cần "camera" theo nghĩa quay phim
**Voice:** giọng Anh-Mỹ rõ ràng, tốc độ vừa-nhanh hơn bản mindfulness trước (đây là nội dung thông tin, không phải thư giãn), dứt khoát nhưng vẫn gần gũi

**Vì sao chọn phong cách này thay vì hình ảnh giống thật:** ngách tài chính khán giả rất nhạy với "fake authority" (người không có thật đóng vai chuyên gia). Dùng hẳn phong cách đồ hoạ/minh hoạ tránh được nghi vấn "đây có phải người thật không", đồng thời né được yêu cầu gắn nhãn AIGC cho phần lớn cảnh (vì không photorealistic).

**Lưu ý pháp lý — nhắc lại:** toàn bộ script dưới đây dừng ở mức nhận thức/tâm lý chi tiêu, KHÔNG đưa khuyến nghị đầu tư, con số lãi suất cụ thể, hay hướng dẫn tài chính cá nhân hoá. Đây không phải tư vấn tài chính.

## Day 1 — "3 spending habits quietly making you poorer"

**Hook (0-4s):** Wallet icon on screen, coins slowly draining out one by one.

**Voice-over:**
> "You're not bad with money. You just have three habits spending in the background before you make a real choice.
> Number one: subscription creep. That's fourteen dollars for a streaming app, nine for a fitness app, five for cloud storage you forgot you even signed up for — until you're paying for five apps you forgot existed.
> Number two: convenience spending. Every 'I'll just order it, I'm tired' feels like ten extra dollars in the moment. But ten extra dollars, four nights a week, quietly becomes more than a hundred and seventy dollars a month.
> Number three: rounding down in your head. You think of an eighteen-dollar coffee run as 'basically fifteen.' Do that four times a week, and you've quietly spent more than three hundred dollars a month on 'basically nothing.'
> None of these make you careless. They just run quietly in the background. This week, try naming just one of them out loud. That's it. Naming it is the first habit you break."

**Caption:**
"Not about being 'bad with money' — it's three quiet habits. Which one hit closest? 👇
#MoneyHabits #PersonalFinance #FinTok #MoneyPsychology"

**Engagement prompt:** "Comment 1, 2, or 3 — which habit is yours?"

**Sound:** Trending "explainer/listicle" background beat — check TikTok Creative Center for a subtle, low-key beat under 20% volume; avoid anything too upbeat, this niche performs better with a calmer, "trustworthy" audio bed.

**AIGC label:** Not required (flat-design/isometric graphics, no photorealistic elements).

**Length target:** ~70s.

## Day 2 — "Why you feel broke even when you're not"

**Hook (0-4s):** Two identical paychecks animate on screen — one from "5 years ago," one from "today."

**Voice-over:**
> "Here's something that messes with almost everyone: you can get a raise, and still feel broke six months later. That's called lifestyle creep."
> "It's not one big decision. It's a slightly nicer apartment — two hundred dollars more a month because 'I can afford it now.' It's upgrading from a used car to a new lease. It's ordering the appetizer AND dessert instead of just picking one, because why not."
> "Each choice feels small and reasonable in the moment. But stack them up, and your spending rises exactly as fast as your income — sometimes faster. Think about it. That promotion was supposed to give you breathing room, but instead, it just gave you a more expensive baseline to maintain. You're making twenty percent more, but saving exactly the same amount."
> "The fix isn't depriving yourself. It's just noticing: did my spending go up because I actually wanted this, or because I simply had the money sitting there?"
> "That one question, asked honestly, catches more leaks than any budgeting app."

**Caption:**
"Getting a raise and still feeling broke isn't bad luck. It's lifestyle creep. Here's what that actually means.
#MoneyHabits #LifestyleCreep #FinTok #PersonalFinance"

**Engagement prompt:** "Has this happened to you? Tell me the moment you noticed it."

**Sound:** Same calm explainer bed style as Day 1 — keeping consistent audio identity across the week helps viewers recognize the series in their feed.

**AIGC label:** Not required.

**Length target:** ~65s.

## Day 3 — "The 'girl math' trap — what it's really costing you"

**Hook (0-4s):** Text bubble animation: "It's basically free if I return the other thing" pops up, then a counter ticks upward.

**Voice-over:**
> "You've probably heard 'girl math' — if I paid in cash it's free, if it's under twenty dollars it doesn't count. It's funny. It's also quietly expensive."
> "The joke works because it's true for almost everyone, not just one group. Our brains are wired to round small numbers down to basically nothing. A seven-dollar iced coffee doesn't feel like spending. A twelve-dollar phone case 'doesn't count' because it was an accident purchase. We mentally write off these small amounts because facing them feels like too much work. But those invisible twenty-dollar swipes can easily drain three or four hundred dollars a month from your account, completely untracked."
> "The problem isn't the joke. It's when the joke becomes the actual system you use to decide what to buy."
> "Try this instead: for one week, write down every 'it doesn't really count' purchase in one place. Not to guilt yourself — just to see the real number."
> "Most people are shocked not by one purchase, but by what all the 'free' ones add up to by Sunday."

**Caption:**
"'Girl math' is funny because it's true. Here's what it's actually costing most of us.
#MoneyHabits #GirlMath #FinTok #MoneyPsychology"

**Engagement prompt:** "What's your go-to 'it basically doesn't count' purchase? Be honest 😅"

**Sound:** If a "girl math" audio trend is active that week, this is the one day worth jumping on it directly — check trend relevance before Day 3 specifically.

**AIGC label:** Not required.

**Length target:** ~65s.

## Day 4 — "The real reason you buy things you don't need"

**Hook (0-4s):** Brain icon animates, a small "stress" cloud appears, then an arrow points to a shopping bag icon.

**Voice-over:**
> "Emotional spending isn't a willpower problem. It's a wiring problem."
> "When you're stressed, bored, or exhausted, buying something gives your brain a real, measurable hit of relief — for about ten minutes. Maybe it's a late-night online order, maybe it's a 'treat yourself' coffee run after a hard meeting."
> "That's not weakness. That's biology. The issue is that the relief is short, and the bill isn't. You are essentially paying a premium just to regulate your mood. The problem is, once the package arrives, the stress of paying for it often outweighs the ten minutes of comfort it originally bought you. It becomes a cycle of seeking relief and then feeling guilty about the cost."
> "Here's a habit that actually works: before you check out, ask 'what am I feeling right now?' — not 'do I need this.' Half the time, naming the feeling — tired, bored, anxious — is enough to make the urge pass on its own, right there in the checkout screen."
> "You don't need more discipline. You need one honest question before the checkout button."

**Caption:**
"Emotional spending isn't about willpower. It's about what happens in your brain before you even open the app.
#MoneyHabits #EmotionalSpending #FinTok #MoneyPsychology"

**Engagement prompt:** "What do you usually buy when you're stressed? No judgment here."

**Sound:** Calm explainer bed, consistent with the week.

**AIGC label:** Not required.

**Length target:** ~70s.

## Day 5 — "Subscription creep — the habit nobody checks"

**Hook (0-4s):** A grid of small app icons fills the screen, each with a tiny price tag.

**Voice-over:**
> "Quick challenge: without looking, guess how many subscriptions you're currently paying for. Now go check your bank statement."
> "Most people guess three or four — maybe streaming and one app. Most people actually have eight to twelve, once you count cloud storage, a forgotten trial that converted to paid, a fitness app you used twice in January."
> "Subscriptions are designed to be forgettable — that's not an accident, that's the business model. The moment you stop noticing the charge, it becomes permanent income for someone else. Companies literally bank on your friction. They know that even if you realize you aren't using an app, the five minutes it takes to find the cancel button often feels like too much effort on a busy Tuesday. So you put it off, and they get paid for another month."
> "Here's the habit: once a month, open your statement and just look at every recurring charge for ten seconds each. Not to cancel everything — just to see it."
> "You don't have to be extreme about this. You just have to actually look."

**Caption:**
"Guess your subscription count, then go check your statement. Most people are off by double.
#MoneyHabits #SubscriptionCreep #FinTok #PersonalFinance"

**Engagement prompt:** "Comment your real number after you check — bet it's higher than you guessed."

**Sound:** Calm explainer bed, consistent with the week.

**AIGC label:** Not required.

## Day 6 — "Why saving money feels impossible"

**Hook (0-4s):** A piggy bank icon animates, coins bounce off instead of going in.

**Voice-over:**
> "If saving money feels impossible, it's probably not a math problem. It's a scarcity mindset problem."
> "When money has felt tight before — even years ago, even a stressful semester in college — your brain can get stuck treating every dollar like it might be the last one for a while. That makes saving feel unsafe, not smart."
> "That's why 'just spend less' advice doesn't work for a lot of people. It's not addressing the actual fear underneath the spending — it's not that they don't know saving is good, it's that saving doesn't FEEL safe. Your brain sees holding onto cash in your checking account as a survival mechanism, while moving it to a savings account feels like it's gone forever. It feels like a loss, not a gain."
> "A small habit that helps: start with an amount so small it feels almost pointless — even five dollars a week. The goal isn't the amount. It's teaching your brain that money going into savings doesn't mean losing it."
> "Once that feels safe, the amount can grow. But safety has to come first."

**Caption:**
"If saving feels impossible, it's not about math. Here's what's actually going on.
#MoneyHabits #ScarcityMindset #FinTok #MoneyPsychology"

**Engagement prompt:** "Does saving feel unsafe to you, or just boring? Curious what it is for most of you."

**Sound:** Calm explainer bed, consistent with the week.

**AIGC label:** Not required.

**Length target:** ~75s.

**Voice-over:**

> "Five different habits. One thing in common — none of them are about being bad with money. They're all about not noticing. Not one of these needed a spreadsheet to fix. Just one honest question, asked at the right moment. We tend to overcomplicate our finances because we think the solution has to be as painful as the problem. But the truth is, mastering your money usually starts with mastering your attention. Awareness is the ultimate budget. It doesn't restrict you; it just forces you to be intentional about where your energy is going."
> "So here's the recap challenge: pick just ONE of these five, and ask yourself the one honest question that goes with it, today. Not all five. Just one."

## Ghi chú sản xuất chung

- **Độ dài:** mỗi video nhắm 60-90 giây để đủ điều kiện Creator Rewards Program (video < 60s không được tính thưởng dù bao nhiêu view).
- **Watch-through rate quan trọng hơn cả:** vì đây là nội dung giáo dục hơi dài hơn dạng mindfulness ngắn trước đây, hook 4 giây đầu phải thật mạnh (số liệu bất ngờ hoặc câu hỏi gây tò mò) để giữ chân người xem qua giây thứ 5 — nếu không, watch-through rate thấp sẽ kéo RPM xuống dù đúng ngách finance.
- **Subtitle bắt buộc, không phải tuỳ chọn:** với nội dung giải thích nhiều thông tin, phần lớn người xem TikTok tắt tiếng lúc lướt — subtitle rõ ràng quyết định việc họ dừng lại hay lướt qua.
- **Không cần đổi PromptGenerator quá nhiều trong PauseFlow** — chỉ cần đổi `style_bible.txt` sang bản flat-design ở trên và `brand_voice.md` sang tông "explainer thẳng thắn, dứt khoát" thay vì "chậm rãi, thì thầm" — pipeline 8 bước giữ nguyên.
- **Không đưa lời khuyên đầu tư/con số lãi suất cụ thể** ở bất kỳ ngày nào — giữ đúng ranh giới "nhận thức tâm lý chi tiêu", tránh rủi ro bị xem là tư vấn tài chính không giấy phép.

# PHẦN 3 — Brand Voice (giọng văn + ranh giới nội dung cho ScriptAgent)

# Brand Voice — "Money Habits"

## Niche & positioning
Personal finance **psychology** for a US TikTok audience — spending habits, money mindset, emotional/behavioral patterns. NOT investment advice, NOT specific financial guidance, NOT numbered interest rates or product recommendations.

## Tone
- Direct, honest, slightly conversational — like a smart friend explaining something, not a lecture.
- Confident and a little faster-paced than a relaxation/mindfulness voice — this is explainer content, not ASMR.
- Non-judgmental: never shame the viewer for their spending habits. Reframe "bad with money" as "quiet, unnoticed habits."
- Uses concrete, relatable scenarios (a $9 forgotten subscription, an $18 coffee run) instead of abstract advice.

## Structure every script should follow
1. **Hook (0-4s):** a surprising number, a relatable scenario, or a direct question.
2. **3 (or fewer) concrete points**, each grounded in a specific everyday example.
3. **One small, doable action** — never a full "budget overhaul." The action should feel almost too small to matter (e.g. "name one habit out loud," "save $5 this week").
4. **One honest closing question** that invites a comment — this is the primary engagement driver.

## Hard boundaries (never cross these)
- No investment advice, no stock/crypto recommendations, no specific interest rates or return numbers.
- No claims of guaranteed outcomes ("this will save you $X/month").
- No medical/clinical language about money anxiety — describe behavior, not diagnosis.
- Always close with "not financial advice" framing implicit in the content itself (awareness-level only, never prescriptive).

## Engagement pattern
- Every single day includes a comment-prompting question — never skip this, even on informational days.
- Day 7 of each week is a recap that explicitly references the other 6 days' icons/topics, and sets up anticipation for "next week."

## Length & pacing
- Target 60-90 seconds per video (Creator Rewards Program requires ≥60s to qualify for payout).
- Hook must be strong enough to survive the first 5 seconds, since watch-through rate below that threshold hurts RPM regardless of niche.

# PHẦN 4 — Spec kỹ thuật pipeline PauseFlow (dùng chung cho cả 2 dự án)

# PauseFlow — Automated AI Video Pipeline Spec
### Cho dự án "Pause to Breathe" (TikTok/Reels thị trường Mỹ)

---

## 1. Mục tiêu

Người dùng chỉ cần nhập **chủ đề hoặc script thô** (ví dụ: "Ngày hôm nay tôi thấy quá tải" hoặc một đoạn script có sẵn), chương trình sẽ tự động:

1. Viết/chỉnh sửa script theo đúng giọng văn thương hiệu (Real & Raw + Micro-storytelling, tiếng Anh, tông mindfulness/slow-living).
2. Chia script thành các phân cảnh (scene breakdown), mỗi cảnh có hook/nhịp cảm xúc riêng.
3. Với mỗi cảnh, tự quyết định **1-step (text-to-video)** hay **2-step (image-to-video)** dựa trên độ phức tạp của cảnh, rồi sinh prompt tương ứng — áp dụng Style Bible cố định để giữ đồng bộ hình ảnh xuyên suốt các video.
4. Sinh ảnh (nếu 2-step) → sinh video cho từng cảnh qua backend AI video (có thể cấu hình: Kling / Runway Gen-3 / Pika / Veo...).
5. Tạo giọng đọc **tiếng Anh** (chính, cho thị trường Mỹ) bằng TTS API — có toggle chuyển sang tiếng Việt (Vbee API) nếu sau này cần bản song ngữ.
6. Tự động tạo subtitle (căn thời gian khớp với voice-over).
7. Gắn **metadata nhãn AI-generated content** cho từng cảnh (đúng chính sách TikTok).
8. Dùng **FFmpeg** ghép video + voice + subtitle + nhạc nền (chọn từ thư viện sound trending hoặc nhạc mộc mặc định) → xuất file MP4 1080x1920 (9:16), sẵn sàng đăng TikTok.

## 5. Luồng xử lý chi tiết + schema dữ liệu

### Bước 1 — ScriptAgent
**Input:** `topic: str` (chủ đề hoặc script thô)
**Output:**
```json
{
  "script": "When was the last time you actually stopped?...",
  "caption": "You don't need a vacation...",
  "hashtags": ["#PauseToBreathe", "#SlowLiving", "#MindfulMoments"],
  "engagement_prompt": "Drop a 🍵 if you needed this today."
}
```
ScriptAgent dùng `brand_voice.md` làm system prompt (giọng Real & Raw, câu ngắn, không lời khuyên y tế) để đảm bảo mọi script mới vẫn đúng chất giọng đã thiết lập ở tuần đầu.

### Bước 2 — SceneSplitter
**Input:** script từ bước 1
**Output:** danh sách 2-4 scene object:
```json
[
  {
    "scene_id": 1,
    "voice_line": "When was the last time you actually stopped?",
    "visual_idea": "close-up hands wrapped around steaming tea cup, morning light",
    "duration_estimate_sec": 4
  }
]
```

### Bước 3 — PromptGenerator
Với mỗi scene, quyết định `mode: "1-step" | "2-step"` dựa trên rule đơn giản:
- Cảnh có nhân vật/đối tượng "hero" lặp lại xuyên thương hiệu (tay, người, đèn lồng cận cảnh) → `2-step` (ảnh trước để kiểm soát bố cục, rồi mới animate).
- Cảnh phụ/chuyển tiếp/hiệu ứng đơn giản (ánh sáng, bokeh, lá cây đung đưa) → `1-step` (text-to-video thẳng).

**Output mỗi scene:**
```json
{
  "scene_id": 1,
  "mode": "2-step",
  "image_prompt": "close-up of hands wrapped around a ceramic tea cup... [+ Style Bible]",
  "video_prompt": "steam slowly rising and drifting from the tea cup..."
}
```
`image_prompt` luôn tự động nối thêm nội dung `assets/style_bible.txt` ở cuối để đảm bảo đồng bộ.

### Bước 4 — MediaGenBackend
- Nếu `mode == "2-step"`: gọi image API → nhận ảnh → gọi video API dạng image-to-video với ảnh đó làm input.
- Nếu `mode == "1-step"`: gọi thẳng text-to-video API với `video_prompt`.
- Retry tối đa 2 lần nếu output lỗi format hoặc tỷ lệ khung sai (phải đúng 9:16).
- Lưu từng clip vào `output/{date}_{topic_slug}/clips/scene_{id}.mp4`.

### Bước 5 — TTSModule
- Input: toàn bộ `voice_line` của các scene ghép lại (hoặc từng câu riêng nếu cần timing chính xác hơn).
- Output: file audio `voice.mp3` + (nếu provider hỗ trợ) word-level timestamps để bước 6 dùng trực tiếp, không cần chạy lại Whisper.
- Cấu hình giọng: Anh-Mỹ, tông trầm, tốc độ chậm (~0.9x mặc định) — khớp tinh thần chậm rãi của thương hiệu.

### Bước 6 — SubtitleGenerator
- Nếu có timestamp từ TTS → dùng trực tiếp để xuất `.srt`.
- Nếu không có → chạy Whisper align voice.mp3 với script gốc để lấy timestamp.
- Mỗi dòng phụ đề giới hạn ~6-8 từ để dễ đọc trên mobile.

### Bước 7 — AIGCLabeler
- Với mỗi scene có `mode == "2-step"` hoặc prompt chứa từ khoá photorealistic → gắn `"aigc_label_required": true` trong `metadata.json`.
- Cảnh dạng minh hoạ/painterly (như kiểu đã đổi ở Ngày 6 trong bản trước) → `"aigc_label_required": false`.
- Xuất cảnh báo rõ ràng ở cuối log: *"⚠️ Nhớ bật toggle AI-generated content khi đăng — X/Y cảnh trong video này cần gắn nhãn."*

### Bước 8 — FFmpegRenderer
Lệnh FFmpeg mẫu (ghép clip theo thứ tự + mix audio + burn subtitle):
```bash
ffmpeg -f concat -safe 0 -i clips_list.txt \
  -i voice.mp3 -i background_music.mp3 \
  -filter_complex "[1:a]volume=1.0[a1];[2:a]volume=0.15[a2];[a1][a2]amix=inputs=2:duration=first[aout]" \
  -map 0:v -map "[aout]" \
  -vf "subtitles=subtitles.srt:force_style='FontSize=18,PrimaryColour=&HFFFFFF&'" \
  -c:v libx264 -crf 18 -preset medium -s 1080x1920 \
  -c:a aac -shortest \
  output/final.mp4
```

## 8. Lưu ý khi đưa cho Antigravity build

- Vì các nhà cung cấp video AI (Kling, Runway, Veo/FlowKit...) có API/cách xác thực khác nhau và có thể thay đổi, nên **ưu tiên thiết kế interface chung trước, code từng backend cụ thể sau** — Antigravity nên implement `media_gen/base.py` xong rồi mới nối API thật.
- Bước 6 (Subtitle) và Bước 5 (TTS) nên tách rõ, vì có thể provider TTS không trả timestamp — cần chỗ fallback (Whisper) rõ ràng trong code, không giả định luôn có sẵn.
- Nhạc nền trending KHÔNG nên tự động hoá (vì thay đổi theo tuần và cần con người chọn cảm quan) — hệ thống chỉ tự ghép nhạc mặc định trong `music_library/`, phần thay nhạc trending để làm thủ công sau khi có bản render.
